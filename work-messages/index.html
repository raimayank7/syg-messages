Everyday work messages

Short, clear messages make work easier for everyone. These cover the moments that come up every week in an office chat.

Welcome a new colleague

  • Welcome to the team, [Name]! Glad to have you with us. Ask anything, any time. All of us were new once.

  • Hello everyone, please welcome [Name], who joins us today as [role]. [Name], we are happy you are here.

  • Welcome aboard! The first week is mostly names and passwords. It gets better. Tea at 4?

Appreciate good work

  • Great work on the presentation, [Name]. The client's reaction said it all.

  • Thank you, team. This week was heavy and nobody dropped the ball. Proud to work with you.

  • [Name], the way you handled that customer call was excellent. Calm, clear and kind.

  • A quick note to say your report was the clearest one I read this month. Thank you for the care you took.

Before going on leave

  • Hi all, I am on leave from [date] to [date]. [Name] is handling my work. For anything urgent, please call. I will check messages once a day.

  • Handover note: files are in the shared folder, pending items are listed in the sheet, and [Name] has the client details. Back on [date].

Asking and declining

  • Hi [Name], could I take 10 minutes of your time today? I need your advice on [topic].

  • I am stuck on [task] and do not want to delay the team. Could someone who has done this before guide me?

  • Thank you for thinking of me. My plate is full this week and I would not do it justice. Can I take it up from Monday?

  • I cannot join the call at that time. Could we do [time] instead? If not, please go ahead and I will read the notes.

Introducing yourself

  • Hello everyone, I am [Name] and I have joined as [role]. I look forward to working with all of you.

  • Hi [Name], I am [Name] from [company]. [Person] suggested I reach out to you about [topic]. Would you have ten minutes this week?

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word, or choose All.

Make it yours

  • One message, one purpose. Do not mix a thank you with a new request.
  • Praise in the group, correct in private.
