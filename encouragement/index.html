Encouragement messages

When someone is low, they do not need a lecture on positivity. They need company. These messages take the person's side first and offer hope second.

After a setback

  • That was a hard blow, and you are allowed to feel it. When you are ready to plan, I am here.

  • One bad result does not cancel years of effort. Rest this week. We start again on Monday.

  • You tried something difficult. Most people never do. I am proud of you for that alone.

  • The door that closed was one door. You have opened harder ones before.

  • Losing the job says something about the company's budget. It says nothing about your worth. Let us update your CV together.

During a long, tiring phase

  • You have been carrying a lot for a long time. It is all right to be tired. You are doing better than you think.

  • You do not have to solve the whole year today. Just today's part.

  • Slow progress is still progress. Keep going at your own speed.

  • I see how hard you are working, even on the days nobody says so.

  • Take a day off from being strong. I will hold things for a bit.

Before something big

  • You have prepared for this. Walk in like you belong there, because you do.

  • Nervous means it matters. Breathe. You will be fine.

  • Whatever happens in that room, you will come out as the same capable person. Go.

When someone feels alone

  • You are not a burden. Call me at any hour. I mean it.

  • I do not have answers, but I have time and tea. Come over.

  • No need to talk. I am just checking that you ate today.

  • एक नाकामी पूरी कहानी नहीं होती। थोड़ा आराम करो, फिर साथ में नई शुरुआत करेंगे।

  • थक गए हो तो रुक जाओ, पर हार मत मानो।

  • तुम्हारी मेहनत मैंने देखी है। नतीजा देर से आएगा, पर आएगा ज़रूर।

  • आज का दिन निकल जाए, बस इतना काफ़ी है। कल की कल देखेंगे।

  • जब मन भारी हो, फ़ोन कर लेना। समय की चिंता मत करना।

  • हिम्मत रखो। रात कितनी भी लंबी हो, सुबह होती ही है।

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word, or choose All.

Make it yours

  • Agree that it is hard before saying it will get better.
  • Do not start with "at least". It makes the problem sound small.
  • Follow up in a few days. The second message matters more than the first.
