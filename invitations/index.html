Short invitation messages

A WhatsApp invitation needs the occasion, date, time, place and how to reply. These are friendly and complete. Replace the words in [brackets].

Birthday party

  • You are invited! [Name] turns [age] on [date]. Join us for cake and dinner at [time] at [place]. Please confirm by [date].

  • Come celebrate [Name]'s birthday with us this [day], [time], at our home. No gifts, only your company and your appetite.

  • It is a surprise party for [Name]! [Date], [time], [place]. Please arrive 15 minutes early, and not a word to the birthday person.

Dinner and get together

  • Dinner at our place this Saturday, 8 pm. Nothing fancy, just good food and long talk. Do come.

  • Old friends, new stories. Get together on [date], [time], at [place]. Reply IN or OUT.

  • Chai and pakoras at ours this Sunday evening, 5 pm. The rain is optional. You are not.

Housewarming and puja

  • With joy we invite you and your family to the griha pravesh of our new home on [date] at [time]. Address: [address]. Lunch will follow the puja.

  • We are having a Satyanarayan puja at home on [date] at [time]. Please join us with your family for the puja and prasad.

  • You are warmly invited to the Sukhmani Sahib path at our home on [date] at [time], followed by langar.

Kitty party, farewell and office

  • Kitty party this month at my place: [date], [time]. Theme: [theme]. Please be on time. The game starts at [time] sharp.

  • Farewell for [Name] on [date] at [time] in the conference room. Please join us to wish them well.

  • Annual day celebration on [date] at [time] in the school hall. Parents are warmly invited. Please be seated by [time].

हिन्दी में

  • आप सादर आमंत्रित हैं। [नाम] के जन्मदिन पर [तारीख] को शाम [समय] बजे हमारे घर पधारें। आपका आना ही सबसे बड़ा उपहार होगा।

  • हमारे नए घर के गृह प्रवेश पर [तारीख] को [समय] बजे सपरिवार पधारकर हमें अनुगृहीत करें। पूजा के बाद भोजन की व्यवस्था है।

  • इस शनिवार रात का खाना हमारे यहाँ। समय: 8 बजे। ज़रूर आइएगा।

  • [तारीख] को हमारे घर [पूजा का नाम] है। आप सपरिवार आमंत्रित हैं। प्रसाद ग्रहण करने अवश्य पधारें।

  • पुराने दोस्तों की महफ़िल: [तारीख], [समय], [जगह]। आना पक्का करो, बहाने नहीं चलेंगे।

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word, or choose All.

Make it yours

  • Send the location pin right after the invitation.
  • Ask for a reply by a date if you are ordering food.
  • For elders, follow the message with a phone call. A call is the real invitation.
